Frequently Asked Questions

Is what I type private?

Your input is sent to our AI service to generate your result. No account, no signup, and nothing is linked to your identity. We count anonymous usage per tool for analytics. Your text is never sold.

Which platforms does this work for?

Shopify, Etsy, Amazon, eBay, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace — any store that accepts plain text or basic HTML.

Is this really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, completely free with no signup and no account. You can generate as many descriptions as you need, one at a time.

Is the output SEO-friendly?

Yes. Descriptions naturally include target keywords when you provide product details and audience.

Do I own the copy?

Yes. Anything you generate is yours to use commercially — no attribution needed.

Which tone should I choose?

"Persuasive" is the default and works for most products. Use "luxurious" for premium items, "playful" for lifestyle/kids, "technical" for electronics, and "minimal" for design-focused stores.

How do I write a product description that sells?

Lead with the customer benefit instead of the feature — "keeps coffee hot for 12 hours" beats "double-wall vacuum insulation." Match your store's voice, address the shopper's problem, and close with a call to action. The generator follows this structure for you.

How long should a product description be?

Most e-commerce descriptions perform best at 100 to 250 words. Use the Short preset (80-120 words) for marketplaces like Amazon and eBay, and the Long preset (180-250 words) when SEO matters on your own store.

What should every product description include?

The product name, a benefit-driven opening hook, key features translated into benefits, concrete specs (sizes, materials, compatibility), who it is for, and a call to action. Enter the raw details above and the generator assembles all of it.

Will AI-generated descriptions hurt my search rankings?

No — search engines reward unique, useful copy and penalize thin or duplicate content. Each description you generate from different product details is unique. Avoid pasting the same output across multiple listings; generate per-product copy instead.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Describe your product. Paste the product name and its key features into the box above: materials, sizes, specs, what makes it different. Bullet points work fine — the AI does the prose.
  2. Pick a tone, audience, and length. Choose from five tones (persuasive, luxurious, playful, technical, minimal), name who you are selling to, and pick a length preset. Short fits marketplace listings; long gives your own store more SEO weight.
  3. Hit Generate Description. Your prompt is sent to our AI service, which writes original copy structured around a benefit-first hook, features framed as benefits, and a closing call to action. Hit Try Sample first if you want to see a realistic run before typing your own.
  4. Copy it into your store. Use the Copy button, paste the description into your product page, and give it a quick read for accuracy before publishing. Tweak any line in your own voice — it is your copy.

Why Product Descriptions Drive Revenue

Product descriptions are the unsung hero of e-commerce. Studies consistently show that the vast majority of online shoppers rate product descriptions as extremely important when making purchase decisions — often more important than customer reviews or product images. A weak description loses sales. A strong one converts browsers into buyers.

The problem is scale. If you run a Shopify store with 200 products, writing unique, SEO-optimized descriptions for each one takes weeks. And generic, manufacturer-provided descriptions hurt your search rankings because Google flags duplicate content. You need unique copy for every product — and that is exactly what this tool provides.

Our AI Product Description Generator analyzes your product name, key features, and target audience to produce compelling copy that follows proven e-commerce copywriting formulas. It opens with a benefit-driven hook, addresses the customer's problem, highlights key features with emotional language, and closes with a call to action.

What Makes Great Product Copy

Lead with Benefits, Not Features: "Keeps your coffee hot for 12 hours" beats "double-wall vacuum insulation." The AI understands this distinction and frames technical specs as customer benefits.

Match Your Brand Voice: Choose between persuasive, luxurious, playful, technical, or minimal tone to match your store's personality. The same product gets very different descriptions depending on whether you are selling to enterprise buyers or Gen Z consumers.

SEO-Ready Output: Every description naturally incorporates the product name and category terms that shoppers actually search for, helping your product pages rank without keyword stuffing.
