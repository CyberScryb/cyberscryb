CyberScryb's AI Writing Suite analyzes your text with 9 linguistic heuristics to detect patterns commonly found in AI-generated content. Unlike other detectors, we show you exactly which patterns were detected and why each sentence was flagged — so you can learn and improve your writing.

Detection runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server. No signup, no limits, completely free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI detector accurate?

Our detector uses 9 linguistic heuristics — including complexity variance, burstiness, phrase density, and vocabulary richness — to identify statistical patterns common in AI-generated text. It provides transparent analysis, not a black-box verdict.

Does it save my text?

No. Detection runs 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never transmitted to any server. Auto-save uses localStorage on your device.

Can I fix flagged sentences?

Yes! Click any highlighted sentence to see exactly why it was flagged and get specific improvement suggestions. For AI-powered rewriting, upgrade to Pro.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. Detection, readability, tone, and repetition analysis are all free with no limits. Pro features like AI-powered sentence rewriting are available with a subscription.
