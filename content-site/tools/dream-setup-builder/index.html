        How the estimate works
        
        
        
          Each card is a type of thing, not a product. A 27-inch screen at 75 Hz is a size and a
          refresh rate. A mesh chair with lumbar is a kind of chair. The low and high numbers are
          round planning figures so a starter budget and a dream budget can sit next to each other.
          They will not match a cart, and they are not an offer.
        
        
        
        
          The ergonomics score watches five things. Monitor height and the chair count the most,
          then keyboard placement, then the pointer and the light. A laptop lid scores low because
          the screen sits below your eyes. A separate keyboard scores higher because the screen can
          come up. A drafting stool scores higher only when the desk can rise. The tips under the
          meter say what to do with the pieces you picked.
        
        
        
        
          Must-have and nice-to-have change with the use. A coding desk wants a screen, a chair, a
          real surface, and a separate keyboard. A creator desk wants light, a voice, and a camera.
          Leaving a must-have on “what you already have” keeps it on the open list. The vibe is a
          nickname for the mix, from Blank slate to Greenhouse loft. It is not advice about a
          purchase.
        
        
        
        
          Price ranges are rough planning estimates, not quotes from a store. Real prices move, and
          nothing here is a product you can buy from this page.
        
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Questions
        
        
        
          Does this send my setup anywhere?
          
        
            No. The page runs in your browser and does not upload the picks. The only save is this
            browser's local storage, and Clear removes it. You can confirm there is no request from
            the tool in the network panel.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          Are the prices real?
          
        
            No. Every range is a rough planning estimate so you can see the shape of a budget. It is
            not a quote, not a shelf price, and not tied to a brand or a store. Nothing on this page
            is for sale.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          How is the ergonomics score made?
          
        
            Monitor height is 30 percent, the chair is 30, keyboard placement is 20, the pointer is
            10, and light is 10. A drafting stool scores higher when the desk can rise. The tips
            under the score cover screen height, the chair, and where the keyboard sits. It is a
            guide, not a medical exam.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          What is the vibe?
          
        
            A short label from the pieces you picked, such as Quiet terminal or Greenhouse loft. It
            is playful. It is not a grade and it does not tell you what to buy.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          What is a must-have versus a nice-to-have?
          
        
            Each use has a short list. Work from home cares about the screen, chair, desk, light,
            and a camera. Gaming cares about the screen, chair, mouse, and audio. A must-have still
            open means you are still on the version you already have. A nice-to-have can wait.
          
        
        
        
        
        
          Where does the setup live, and what does Clear do?
          
        
            Only in this browser. Change a pick and it replaces the saved setup. Clear deletes that
            save and returns the desk to the pieces you already have. Clearing site data for
            cyberscryb.com removes it too. The page does not ask for an account.
