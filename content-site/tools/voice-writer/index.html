Frequently Asked Questions

Is what I type private?

Your input is sent to our AI service to generate your result. No account, no signup, and nothing is linked to your identity. We count anonymous usage per tool for analytics. Your text is never sold.

What is an AI voice writer?

It generates content in a specific tone or register. Instead of generic AI output, you choose the voice — conversational, educational, or strategic — and it writes in that style consistently.

What are the three voice modes?

Conversational writes like you're talking to a smart friend — short sentences, direct, and engaging. Educational writes like a sharp instructor — structured, clear. Strategic writes like a business operator — framework-driven, systematic, result-focused.

What can I use this for?

Social media posts, blog intros, newsletter sections, Reddit posts, email drafts, LinkedIn content — anything where tone matters and you want a starting point you can actually use without rewriting from scratch.

Is it free?

Yes. 100% free to use. No account or credit card required.

Is my content stored?

Inputs are sent to our backend and Google Gemini for generation. CyberScryb does not intentionally store prompts or results in our application database or use them to train our own models. Provider processing and retention follow the policies described on our privacy page. Some tools save drafts locally on your device.

How do I make AI writing sound like me?

Give the tool specifics: your audience, one opinion you hold, and the rough idea in your own words. Pick the closest voice mode, then use the refinement box to push the draft toward your phrasing. The draft is a starting point; a pass in your own voice at the end is what makes it sound like you.

Which voice should I choose for social media posts?

Conversational. It writes in short, direct sentences that read naturally in a feed. Educational fits explainers and threads; strategic fits thought-leadership posts and announcements.

Can I use the output commercially?

Yes. Anything you generate here is yours to use, edit, and publish, including in commercial work. As with any AI draft, read it before you publish and fix any factual claims against your own sources.

Is there a character or usage limit?

The topic field accepts up to 4,000 characters, and generation is unlimited and free. If a draft cuts off, shorten the brief or generate in parts.

How it works

  1. Pick a voice. Conversational reads like talking to a smart friend; educational reads like a sharp instructor; strategic reads like a business operator with a framework.
  2. Drop in your topic. A title, a rough idea, or a few bullet points all work. The more specific the brief, the closer the first draft lands.
  3. Generate and refine. Read the draft, then use the refinement box to steer it: make it shorter, add an example, shift the tone. Repeat until it sounds like you.

Where each voice fits

Conversational Tweets, Reddit posts, casual LinkedIn posts, personal newsletter sections, blog intros that need a pulse.
Educational How-to explainers, tutorial intros, documentation drafts, study notes, anything that needs to teach clearly.
Strategic Business plans, marketing copy, email sequences, project briefs, founder updates, proposals with a point.
