Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI voice writer?

It generates content in a specific tone or register. Instead of generic AI output, you choose the voice — conversational, educational, or strategic — and it writes in that style consistently.

What are the three voice modes?

Conversational writes like you're talking to a smart friend — short sentences, direct, no fluff. Educational writes like a sharp instructor — structured, clear. Strategic writes like a business operator — framework-driven, systematic, result-focused.

What can I use this for?

Social media posts, blog intros, newsletter sections, Reddit posts, email drafts, LinkedIn content — anything where tone matters and you want a starting point you can actually use without rewriting from scratch.

Is it free?

Yes. 3 free generations per day after providing your email. No credit card required.

Is my content stored?

No. Your input is processed in real time and discarded. We never store or train on your content.
