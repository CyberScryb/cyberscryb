What is the character limit for tweets?

Twitter/X allows 280 characters per tweet. Our generator keeps each tweet within that limit by default so you can copy and post directly.

Absolutely. The generated content works great on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon, and Bluesky. Adjust the angle to match the platform's tone.

Viral tweets typically have a strong hook in the first line, offer a contrarian or surprising take, and end with a call to action. Our "hot take" angle is optimized for engagement.
