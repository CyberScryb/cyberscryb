Meetings End. The Work Evaporates.

Everyone has lived this: a good meeting, real decisions, clear next steps — and by Thursday nobody remembers who owned what. The notes sit in a doc nobody reopens. The action items lived in someone's head until they didn't. The cost is not the meeting. It is the re-meeting.

This tool closes that gap in about ten seconds. Paste whatever you have — a transcript, bullet notes, a rambling voice memo transcription — and get back the four things a meeting actually owes you: what it was about, what was decided, who owns what by when, and the follow-up email that makes it all official. No bot joining your calls. No signup. No subscription.

One rule the tool follows strictly: it only uses what is in your notes. If an owner or deadline was never stated, it says "not stated" instead of inventing one. An action list you can trust beats a polished one you can't.

How It Works

Step 1: Paste your notes. Transcript, bullets, chaos — all fine. Add the meeting title if you want it labeled.

Step 2: Pick your detail level. Concise for a quick recap, detailed when you need the follow-up email draft too.

Step 3: Get the recap. Summary, decisions, action items with owners, open questions.

Step 4: Send it. Copy the follow-up into Slack or email before the next meeting starts and watch the re-meeting disappear.

Real Ways People Use It

Team standups and syncs: Turn the weekly call into an action list before anyone closes the tab.

Client calls: Decisions and commitments, documented while they are fresh, in language you can forward.

Missed meetings: Paste a colleague's notes and get the recap in seconds instead of a 40-minute recording.

1:1s: Keep a running record of what you and your manager actually agreed to.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn meeting notes into action items?

Paste your notes or transcript. The AI returns a summary, the decisions made, and action items with owners and due dates where stated, plus a follow-up email draft.

Does it invent action items that were not discussed?

No. It uses only what is in your notes, and marks unstated owners or dates as not stated instead of guessing.

What kind of notes can I paste?

Anything: transcripts, bullet notes, voice-memo transcriptions. The messier the input, the more the structuring helps.

Is this meeting notes summarizer free?

Yes. 100% free with no accounts, no signup, and no meeting bot to install.

Is what I paste kept private?

Your input is sent securely to our AI service to generate the summary and is not stored after the request completes. Do not paste confidential company information.

How is this different from a generic summarizer?

It separates decisions from discussion, extracts action items with owners, flags open questions, and drafts the follow-up email — the four things you need after a meeting.
