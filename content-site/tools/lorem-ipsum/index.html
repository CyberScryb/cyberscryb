What Is Lorem Ipsum and Why Do Designers Use It?

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used in graphic design, web development, and publishing to fill space before real content is available. The text is derived from a scrambled passage of Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, written in 45 BC. Richard McClintock, a Latin professor at Hampden-Sydney College, identified the source in 1994. The text has been used as standard placeholder content since the 1500s, when an unknown printer scrambled a galley of type to make a type specimen book.

The reason designers prefer lorem ipsum over real text, or even random words, is that it looks like real written language without drawing attention to itself. When a reader encounters recognizable words, their eye naturally tries to read them, pulling focus away from the visual layout. Lorem ipsum neutralizes that problem — it has the rhythm and visual density of natural text without triggering the reading reflex.

When to Use Placeholder Text

Placeholder text is useful in three specific situations. First, during wireframing and prototyping, when the design needs to show how text will flow through a layout before copywriters have written anything. Second, during front-end development, when building a component that will eventually receive real content from a CMS or API. Third, in mockups and presentations to clients, where you want the visual design to communicate clearly without the client getting distracted by half-finished copy.

You should stop using placeholder text the moment real content is available. Lorem ipsum is never meant to ship in production. It creates accessibility problems for screen readers, confuses users who encounter it, and is often flagged by content auditing tools as an error. Keep it in development environments only.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between paragraphs, sentences, and words?

Paragraphs generate full blocks of placeholder text, typically 4-8 sentences each — useful for body content layouts. Sentences generate shorter, single-sentence units for captions or subheadings. Words give you individual tokens for testing labels, tags, or tight UI components.

Is lorem ipsum a real language?

No. It is scrambled Latin derived from a philosophical text. The standard lorem ipsum passage is not grammatically correct Latin — individual words are real Latin words, but the passage as a whole is deliberately scrambled to prevent meaning from interfering with design evaluation.

Can I use this text in a client deliverable?

In a mockup or prototype shown to a client, yes — it is the standard practice and clients understand what placeholder text means. You should never ship lorem ipsum text in a live product. Replace all placeholder content before launch.
