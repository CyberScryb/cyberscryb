Why Most Performance Reviews Fail the Person Reading Them

Most reviews fail before the meeting starts. The manager writes from memory of the last six weeks, reaches for trait language because it is fast, and produces a document the employee cannot act on. Great attitude. Needs to communicate better. Neither sentence tells anyone what happened, what it caused, or what changes on Monday.

A defensible review is built on observable behavior only. What did the person deliver, miss, fix, or break, and what was the impact? Every claim carries an example the employee recognizes as true. That is slower to write by hand, which is exactly why reviews get rushed in November and December when year-end season hits.

This writer takes your rough notes, the specific examples only you know, and structures them into a complete review: summary tied to the rating, strengths with evidence, growth areas with behavior impact and next step, and SMART goals for next period. If your notes are too vague to support a claim, it flags that instead of padding. You stay the author. It does the structuring you never have time for.

How It Works

Step 1: Pick the mode. Manager review if you are writing about a direct report. Self-review if you are writing about your own period.

Step 2: Add the basics. First name or initials, role, review period, and the overall rating. The narrative will be written to justify that rating, and flagged if your examples contradict it.

Step 3: Paste your notes. Strengths with concrete examples, growth areas with what actually happened, and goals for next period. Specific notes in, specific review out. Vague notes get flagged, not fluffed.

Step 4: Edit before it counts. Read it as the employee. Cut anything you could not defend with an example, fix any bracketed placeholders, then use it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write a self-review?

Start from outcomes, not effort. List what you delivered with numbers attached, tie each result to a team or business goal, name one honest growth area with the step you are already taking, and close with specific goals for next period. Avoid modesty that hides real work and avoid trait claims like hard worker. Use observable results instead.

What should I say in a performance review?

Say what the person observably did, the impact it had, and what changes next. Every strength should carry a concrete example, a project, a metric, or an incident. Every growth area should name the behavior, the impact, and one concrete change to make. Ratings only feel fair when the words underneath them match.

What are good performance review phrases?

Phrases built on observable behavior: consistently delivered X ahead of deadline, reduced Y by Z percent, took ownership of a failing process and stabilized it, needs to flag blockers earlier because two launches slipped without warning. Avoid trait phrases like great attitude or team player, which say nothing defensible.

How do I write a performance review for an employee?

Open with a summary that matches the rating, then give strengths with examples, growth areas with behavior impact and next step, SMART goals for next period, and a short forward-looking close. Never introduce a problem in the review that was never raised during the period. Surprises are a management failure, not feedback.
