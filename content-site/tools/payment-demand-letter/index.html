What actually works

Tone ladder

Friendly → firm → final. Jumping straight to threats burns bridges and can look bad if you later need a judge. Match mode to how many prior contacts you already made.

One clear ask

Every demand letter needs amount, reference, original due date, and a new pay-by date. Ambiguity is why invoices age.

Only real next steps

Never invent lawsuits, “collections tomorrow,” or criminal claims. State only actions you are willing and able to take.

Keep the trail

Save invoices, SOWs, and each reminder. Small claims and collections start with documents, not volume of anger.

FAQ

Will this guarantee payment?

No. A clear paper trail improves odds and prepares you if you escalate.

Should I use certified mail?

Useful for final notices and larger amounts. Email is fine for friendly reminders if that is how you already work.

Late fees?

Only charge late fees if your contract or invoice terms allow them. Don’t invent penalties.

Is this legal advice?

No. For large amounts or disputes, consult a lawyer or small-claims resources in your area.
