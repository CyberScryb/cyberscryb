How to Send Certified Mail for Gym Cancellation

1. Print & Sign Two Copies

Print this letter. Sign and date both copies in ink. Keep one signed copy for your permanent records alongside your membership contract.

2. USPS Certified Mail + Green Card

At the post office, request USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested (PS Form 3811 green card). This provides legal proof of delivery date.

3. Stop Payment with Your Bank

If the club bills after the effective date, provide your bank with a copy of this letter and certified delivery proof under Federal Regulation E.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't gyms allow easy online cancellations?

Many health clubs rely on "friction-based retention" — making cancellation deliberately cumbersome so members continue paying monthly dues. While the Federal Trade Commission's "Click to Cancel" rule aims to simplify cancellations, sending a formal certified letter remains the most legally bulletproof method to terminate memberships immediately under state health club statutes.

What is Federal Regulation E and how does it protect me?

Under Federal Regulation E (12 CFR § 1005.10(c)), you have the statutory right to stop preauthorized electronic fund transfers (ACH debits) at any time. By sending written revocation to the gym and submitting proof to your bank, your financial institution is legally obligated to block any unauthorized debits.

How does the 25-mile relocation rule work?

Most state Health Club Services Acts require fitness clubs to allow cancellation if a member permanently moves more than 25 miles away from the home facility without an affiliated facility nearby. Attaching a utility bill, new lease, or driver's license showing your new address satisfies this statutory requirement.
