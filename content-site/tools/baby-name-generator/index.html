Matching Beats Listing

Static name lists all suffer the same flaw: they ignore the only two things that determine whether a name actually works — the surname it lands on and the siblings it stands next to. A beautiful first name that rhymes with the last name is a joke that lasts a lifetime.

This tool does the matching work for you. Every suggestion is checked against your surname for rhythm, repeated sounds, and rhyming traps, and against your other children's names for a harmony that keeps each kid's identity distinct. Each name comes with its real origin and meaning — no invented meanings, no fake etymology — and a plain-English note on exactly why it fits.

It will not invent names, and it will tell you when a meaning is uncertain rather than making one up. Personalization is the pitch; honesty is the product.

How It Works

Step 1: Set the brief. Gender, style, and origin — narrow it down or leave it wide open.

Step 2: Add your surname. This is where the flow matching happens: cadence, clashing sounds, and initial-checking.

Step 3: Add sibling names. The generator keeps the new name in the same register while giving each child their own sound.

Step 4: Shortlist from ten. Ten names with meanings and fit notes — say each one out loud with the surname before you commit.

Real Ways People Use It

First-time parents: Style-guided shortlists with meanings, so the name you pick actually means something.

Sibling matching: A name that sits naturally next to your other kids' names without sounding like a copy.

Heritage picks: Filter by origin to honor family roots, with the real meaning beside each name.

Surname traps: Catching the rhymes, alliteration clashes, and unfortunate initials before the birth certificate does.
