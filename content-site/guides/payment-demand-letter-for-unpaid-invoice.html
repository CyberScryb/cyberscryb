Payment Demand Letter for Unpaid Invoices

Most late invoices clear after one clear written demand. Lead with the invoice number, amount, original due date, and a new deadline. Escalate tone only after the first notice is ignored.

Friendly → firm → final

Start polite and assume good faith. Second notice restates facts and late policy. Final notice states last chance before collections or small claims — still professional, never abusive.

What to attach

Original invoice PDF, statement of work or contract excerpt, delivery proof, and a payment link or wire instructions.

FAQ

How long should I wait between demand letters?

Seven to fourteen days is common. Match whatever your contract says for late notices.

Is this a legal demand letter?

It is a professional demand you can send yourself. It is not a substitute for a lawyer if the amount is large or the client disputes the work.
