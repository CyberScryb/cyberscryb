Inside the guide

What you will learn

Why detectors flag human writing

Tools like Turnitin, GPTZero, Copyleaks, and Originality.ai measure statistical patterns, not actual origin. Research has found false positive rates reaching into the high single digits on human-authored work, worse for non-native English writers with clear, structured prose.

How detection actually works

Plain-English breakdown of the two signals detectors measure: perplexity (how predictable your word choices are) and burstiness (how much your sentence lengths vary). Once you see the math, the fixes make sense.

A humanization playbook that works

Four concrete editing passes: outline like a human, break rhythmic monotony, cut hedging filler, and audit robotic transitions. Tactics you can apply to any draft in minutes.

Ready-to-use assets

A rewrite prompt that pushes drafts toward natural rhythm, a banned-word list of AI giveaway phrases with human alternatives, and a client defense email template for when a false accusation lands.

An honest note before you download

What this guide gives you is understanding and craft: the ability to write in a way that reads human because it is human, plus a paper trail when a client questions you.
