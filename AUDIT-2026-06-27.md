## 3. Content plan (8 weeks, AdSense-safe, organic-SEO-first)

**Pillars:** (1) Privacy-first dev tools — tutorials around the 13 indexable tools. (2) Life Tools / hardship & appeal letters — Nathan's CNA wedge. (3) AI writing & detection — humanizer/detector ecosystem. (4) Practical guides solving one search query each (long-tail, low competition).

**Format & length standard:** how-to guides 1,200–2,000 words with a worked example and screenshots/code blocks; comparison posts ("X vs Y") 1,000–1,500 words with a table; tool-paired tutorials 1,500+ words (tutorial body + embedded tool CTA, not tool-first).

**On-page SEO baseline for every new page:** unique `<title>` ≤60 chars with primary keyword near the front; meta description 120–155 chars with a call to action; single `<h1>` matching search intent, `<h2>` per sub-question (mirrors "People Also Ask"); 2-3 contextual internal links to related tools/guides; `Article` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD; canonical + OG/Twitter tags (already standard across the site — keep it that way for new pages).
