Vows Are Hard Because They Matter

Writing your own vows is one of the most meaningful things you will ever do — and one of the most intimidating. You are trying to compress years of love into two minutes of words, in front of everyone you know, while emotional. No wonder so many people freeze at the blank page.

The secret of great vows is not poetry. It is specificity. "I promise to love you forever" is forgettable. "I promise to always save you the corner brownie and to laugh at your puns, even the terrible ones" is unforgettable — because only the two of you could have written it. Great vows are built from real memories and concrete promises.

Our AI Wedding Vow Generator uses Google’s Gemini model to turn your love story into vow drafts in your tone — heartfelt, romantic, funny, or traditional — at the length you need. Use a draft as your foundation, then make it yours: add the inside jokes, the real promises, the details only you two share. Our companion guide walks through the classic vow structure line by line.

How It Works

Step 1: Tell your story. Names, how you met, favorite memories, what you admire, what you want to promise. The more real detail, the more personal the vows.

Step 2: Choose tone and length. Match your ceremony: light and funny for a casual wedding, traditional for a formal one. Two minutes (about 300 words) is the sweet spot.

Step 3: Generate drafts. Get 2 to 3 distinct vow drafts. Read each aloud — vows are spoken, and the ear catches what the eye misses.

Step 4: Make them yours. Combine the best lines, add inside jokes and real promises, and practice until it feels natural. Never read AI words verbatim on the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should wedding vows be?

One to two minutes each — roughly 150 to 300 words. Longer than three minutes and guests start checking watches; shorter than a minute can feel rushed. Match your partner’s length so neither of you feels lopsided.

How personal should vows get?

Specific but tasteful. Inside jokes and real memories are wonderful; deeply private details or anything that would embarrass your partner in front of family are not. When in doubt, ask them.

Should vows be funny or serious?

Match your relationship and your audience. A funny line or two inside heartfelt vows is perfect for most couples. If humor is not natural to you, sincerity always lands.

Can I edit the generated vows?

Please do. The drafts are a foundation — the final vows should be unmistakably yours. Add your memories, your promises, your voice.

Is this wedding vow generator free?

Yes. 100% free with unlimited generations, no accounts, and no signup required.

What if I cry while reading them?

You probably will, and that is fine. Pause, breathe, sip water, and keep going — guests love it. Print your vows in large type so tears do not blur the words.
