Frequently Asked Questions

Is this privacy policy generator free?

Yes, this privacy policy generator is completely free with no signup required. Generate as many policies as you need for your websites and apps.

Is the generated privacy policy legally binding?

The generated policy provides a solid starting point based on common legal requirements for data protection. However, we strongly recommend having a qualified attorney review your privacy policy to ensure it fully meets all applicable laws for your specific situation and jurisdiction.

Does this generator support GDPR and CCPA?

Yes. You can enable GDPR and/or CCPA compliance options, and the generator will include the appropriate legal sections covering user rights under those regulations, including rights to access, deletion, and data portability.

What output formats are available?

The generator supports three output formats: formatted HTML (ready to paste into your website), plain text (for documents or emails), and Markdown (for GitHub, documentation sites, etc.). You can copy to clipboard or download as a file in any format.

What is COPPA and do I need it?

COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) is a US federal law that applies to websites and online services that are directed at children under 13 or that knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If your site may be used by children, enable the COPPA option to include the required Children's Privacy section.

Can I customize the generated policy?

The generated policy is yours to edit freely. Copy or download the output in your preferred format and modify it as needed to match your specific business practices and legal requirements.
