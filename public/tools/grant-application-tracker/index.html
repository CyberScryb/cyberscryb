Who this tracker is for

If you are a small business owner, nonprofit staffer, or freelancer chasing grant money, you already know the real problem is not finding grants — it is keeping the paperwork straight. Deadlines stack up, every portal wants a different login, and the document checklist you swore you finished is half empty. This tracker is built for one person running several applications at once: a five-stage pipeline, a deadline radar that flags anything due within 14 days, per-grant document checklists, and a direct link to each funder's official application page. Everything lives on your device, so there is no account and nothing to leak.

How to use it

  1. Add your opportunities. Pick from the verified Idaho funding dataset (43 grants, loans, microloans, tax incentives, workforce funds, and pitch competitions) using the dropdown on the Pipeline tab.
  2. Move cards through the pipeline. Every application travels saved → drafting → applied → pending → won. Drag or change the stage on each card so you always know where things stand at a glance.
  3. Work each card's checklist. Open a card to find the funder's official portal link, the current deadline, a document checklist (budget narrative, tax docs, logic model, and more), and a notes field for your own reminders.
  4. Watch the deadline radar. Switch to the Deadline radar tab for every upcoming deadline soonest first, with anything due within 14 days flagged, and set personal reminders for key dates.
  5. Submit on the funder's portal. The tracker never files anything for you — click through to the official page, log in, and submit yourself. Then mark the card applied and keep tracking the decision.

Real situations this fits

Applying to several grants in one cycle. Four deadlines in three weeks, each with its own portal and paperwork. The pipeline shows you exactly which ones are still drafts and which are already under review, so nothing gets filed half-finished or missed.

A deadline that keeps slipping. Rolling applications are the ones that quietly expire. Put them in the tracker, set a reminder a month out, and the 14-day radar will flag them before the window closes.

Keeping the documents straight. Budgets, board resolutions, tax returns, letters of support — each card's checklist tracks what you have gathered and what is still missing per grant, with notes for where each file lives.

Reapplying next year. Cards marked won or lost stay in your history, with your notes on what worked. Next cycle you start from a playbook instead of a blank page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the tracker submit applications for me?

No. The tracker organizes your applications, deadlines, documents, and reminders. Every entry links straight to the funder's official application page, where you log in and submit yourself.

Where is my tracking data stored?

Only in your browser, on your device (localStorage). There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. The trade-off: clearing your browser data removes it, and it does not sync between devices.

Where do the funding opportunities come from?

The CyberScryb Idaho funding dataset: 43 verified grants, loans, microloans, tax incentives, workforce training funds, and pitch competitions, each checked against its official page. It is Idaho-focused, not national.

How do deadline reminders work?

Set a reminder date on any upcoming deadline and it is saved on your device. The deadline radar lists everything soonest first and highlights anything due within 14 days so nothing slips.

Can I share a pipeline with my team?

Not directly — your data lives in one browser on one device with no account behind it. For team use, each person runs their own copy, or one person keeps the master pipeline and reports from it.

Are the deadlines guaranteed current?

No — funding rounds change. The dataset was verified October 31, 2026, but always confirm the current round on the funder's official page before you file. Every tracker entry links straight there.
