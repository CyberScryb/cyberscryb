What actually matters

Landlords decide on three questions

Can you pay, will you stay, and will you be easy to manage. Every document in your application should answer one of those three. The readiness review scores your checklist against exactly that: income proof, rental history, and the stability signals in between.

Complete beats fast

An incomplete application sits at the bottom of the pile while a complete one gets decided. Gather the documents before you apply, not after the landlord asks. That is the whole point of the checklist: know what is missing while there is still time to get it.

Pets are a plus when they are presented right

A landlord's worry about a pet is noise, damage, and complaints. A pet resume that states vaccinations, spay/neuter, house-training, and behavior in plain terms answers every worry in one page. Details matter: "55 lb Lab mix, house-trained, quiet in the house" beats "my dog is nice."

Follow up once, politely, then move on

One short follow-up a few business days after applying keeps your name up. A second one a week later is the last. After that, more messages mark you as a problem tenant. The tracker exists so you follow up on time, and stop on time.

FAQ

What documents do I need for a rental application?

Most landlords ask for a government photo ID, proof of income such as recent pay stubs or an offer letter, rental history or a prior landlord contact, and references. Some also want renter insurance, bank statements, or a pet resume. Requirements vary by landlord, so confirm with the listing. The document checklist above tracks each item as HAVE or STILL NEED.

What should I include in a rental application cover letter?

A strong cover letter answers the three questions every landlord asks: can you pay, will you stay, and will you be easy to manage. State your employment and income plainly, describe your rental history in your own terms, and give a positive reason for moving. Our AI Rental Application Letter Writer drafts it from your facts; this companion tool handles the checklist, pet resume, and follow-up.

Do I need a pet resume for renting?

Not always, but in a competitive market a pet resume answers a landlord's unspoken questions: is it vaccinated, spayed or neutered, house-trained, and how does it behave. The pet resume mode writes one from your pet details, and our pet resume guide walks through the full method with an example.

How do I follow up on a rental application?

Wait a few business days after applying, then send one short, polite message restating your interest and asking about the timeline. Follow up once more about a week later if there is no reply, then move on. The tracker above holds each application's follow-up date, and the follow-up message mode drafts the message.

What is the 3x income rule for renting?

Many landlords look for monthly income around three times the rent, but it is a common benchmark, not a law. Some accept lower ratios with a larger deposit, a co-signer, or extra documentation. State your income honestly in your application; this tool never inflates or invents income figures.
