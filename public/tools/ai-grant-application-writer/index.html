What actually matters

Match the funder's language

Every opportunity in the dataset carries its eligibility and purpose. The draft is built around those: a rural infrastructure grant wants jobs and public benefit, a pitch competition wants growth story. Pick the right opportunity first — the finder helps you compare.

Specific use of funds wins

"$25,000 for operations" loses to "$15,000 for a commercial planer, $10,000 for six months of shop rent while onboarding wholesale accounts." Name the items, the amounts, and what changes because of them. The draft only uses what you enter.

Deadlines move — verify

Every dataset entry was verified on October 31, 2026, and rounds close. Before you submit, open the funder's official page from the opportunity card and confirm the deadline, amount, and requirements are current.

Never invent qualifications

Funders verify. The tool drafts strictly from your facts and will not invent revenue, employees, or impact numbers. If a fact is missing, the draft omits it — which tells you what to nail down before applying.

FAQ

Where do the funding opportunities come from?

The CyberScryb Idaho funding dataset: 43 state grants, federal programs, tax incentives, workforce training funds, loans, microloans, pitch competitions, and foundation grants. Each entry's page was opened and verified, and entries are flagged when their latest round has closed. More states are being added.

Does the draft guarantee I get the grant?

No. It writes a draft from the facts you enter. It never predicts approval, never invents qualifications or numbers, and never submits anything. Review and customize every draft before filing.

Can I use it for opportunities outside Idaho?

The picker currently covers Idaho. The writer works for any opportunity — pick the closest match and edit the draft to the real funder's requirements.

Is my business information sent anywhere?

Your prompt is sent to our AI service to generate the draft. Drafts and history are saved only on your device. Nothing is submitted to any funder — you file the application yourself on their official page.
