Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the final, human-readable part of a web address that identifies a page, like "my-awesome-blog-post" in cyberscryb.com/blog/my-awesome-blog-post.

How are accents handled?

Accented characters (é, ü, ñ, å) are transliterated to ASCII equivalents (e, u, n, a) using Unicode NFD normalization, producing clean, universally compatible URLs.

Should I remove stop words?

Removing words like "the", "a", "of", "for" creates shorter, more keyword-dense slugs — generally better for SEO.

Why hyphens instead of underscores?

Google treats hyphens as word separators but underscores as joiners. "my-blog-post" is read as three words; "my_blog_post" as one token. Hyphens are the SEO standard.

Is this tool free and private?

Yes. 100% free, no signup. All processing is client-side in vanilla JavaScript.
