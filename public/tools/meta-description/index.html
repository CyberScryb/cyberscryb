Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal meta description length?

Google typically displays 150-160 characters. Our AI targets 140-160 characters per description to ensure nothing gets cut off in search results.

How do meta descriptions affect SEO?

While not a direct ranking factor, well-written meta descriptions improve click-through rates (CTR) from search results, which can indirectly boost rankings. A compelling description is your ad copy in the SERPs.

Should I include keywords in meta descriptions?

Yes. Google bolds matching keywords in search results, making your listing stand out. Our AI naturally weaves in your primary keyword without keyword stuffing.

Can I use this for every page on my site?

Absolutely. Every page should have a unique meta description. Use this tool for landing pages, blog posts, product pages, and more. Free tier gives you 3 generations per day.

What model powers this tool?

Gemini 3.1 Pro — Google's latest frontier model. It understands SEO best practices and generates descriptions that balance keyword relevance with compelling copy.
