Eight ways of being online

The types are The Lurker, The Tab Hoarder, The Fact-Checker, The Group Chat Glue, The Deep Diver, The Meme Curator, The Digital Minimalist, and The Night Owl Scroller. Each scene leans toward one of them and leaves a smaller mark on two companions. The bars show the whole blend, not only the winner.

  • The Lurker Reads the thread, keeps the thought, and rarely needs the last word.
  • The Tab Hoarder Treats an open browser as a workbench of unfinished thoughts.
  • The Fact-Checker Opens the source, checks the date, and then decides what to repeat.
  • The Group Chat Glue Turns a pile of messages into a plan and makes room for the quiet person.
  • The Deep Diver Follows the footnote until a simple question has a real history.
  • The Meme Curator Keeps the reference and sends it when this exact group will get it.
  • The Digital Minimalist Keeps fewer apps, fewer pings, and a reason for each one that stays.
  • The Night Owl Scroller Does the real browsing after the chats settle and the house is quiet.

What best paired with means

A pairing is chosen ahead of time. It is not second place. The Lurker keeps company with the Night Owl and the Meme Curator. The Tab Hoarder shares a workbench with the Deep Diver. The Fact-Checker and the Digital Minimalist both dislike extra noise. The percent beside a companion is how much of your blend landed there. Your scenes decide the numbers. The friendship was already written.

What the card is, and what it is not

The result is a read of the moves you picked. It does not know your accounts, your name, or what you should buy. Each emblem is drawn with shapes in this page: an eye, a stack of cards, a magnifier, three connected dots, ripples, a little frame, a single circle, a moon. There are no product logos and no borrowed pictures.

Copy gives you one line with the type, the blend, and this page. Download draws that same result on a canvas in the browser and saves a PNG. Neither one sends the answers anywhere. Finish the quiz and this browser keeps the choice ids so you can open the card again. Clear deletes them. The privacy tip is a general habit for that type, not an audit of your devices.

Tech Myth or Fact is a swipe deck of checked claims, and the Password Habit Quiz scores reuse and a second factor without asking for a password. The Digital Footprint Check is the cleanup list, and Screen Time Reality Check turns the hours into a year. Spot the Scam Call and the phishing inbox are the practice rooms for the tricks the privacy tip is pointing at.

FAQ

Does this upload my answers?

No. The quiz runs in your browser and does not send the answers anywhere. The only thing saved is the last completed result, as choice ids in this browser's local storage. Clear removes that saved result.

How does a personality win?

Each scene gives points to the type you pick and a smaller number to the two types it is paired with. The type with the most points is the card. If two types tie, the one you picked more often wins. If that is also a tie, the earlier type in the roster wins. The bars are each type's share of the points, and they add up to 100 percent.

What does best paired with mean?

Two companion types, chosen ahead of time, not a second-place trophy. The Lurker pairs with the Night Owl and the Meme Curator, the Tab Hoarder pairs with the Deep Diver and the Night Owl, and the rest of the roster has its own pair. The percent next to a companion is how much of your blend landed there.

What does the downloaded card contain?

The type name, the blend percent, a short description, the privacy tip, the paired types, the emblem, and the bars. The browser draws it on a canvas and saves a PNG. It does not include a logo you do not own, and it does not send the image anywhere.

Where does the last result live, and what does Clear do?

Only in this browser. Finish a quiz and the result replaces whatever was saved. Clear deletes that saved result from local storage. Clearing site data for cyberscryb.com removes it too. The quiz does not ask for an account.

Is the privacy tip personal advice?

No. It is a general habit matched to the type you landed on, such as not pasting a one-time code into a group chat or waiting until morning for a download. It is not a security audit of your accounts, and it does not look at anything on your device.
