Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a QR code?

Simply type or paste any text, URL, WiFi credentials, or email address into the input field. The QR code generates instantly in your browser — no server needed.

Can I customize QR code colors?

Yes! Use the foreground and background color pickers to customize your QR code's appearance. You can also adjust the size from 128px to 512px.

Is this QR code generator free?

100% free, no signup, no watermarks. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device.

What formats can I download?

You can download your QR code as a PNG image (raster) or SVG file (vector/scalable). SVG is ideal for print materials.
