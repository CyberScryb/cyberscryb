Frequently Asked Questions

Why do caregivers need a timesheet?

Most agencies require signed hour logs for payroll, and many families track hours for billing or tax purposes. A written record protects both the caregiver and the client.

Can I use this for multiple clients?

Yes — each generate call makes a clean sheet. Fill in a new client name and pay period for each person you care for.

What if I work overnight shifts?

Just write the in/out times across days (e.g. In 10:00 PM, Out 6:00 AM) and total the hours in the row.

Disclaimer: This is a documentation aid, not legal or payroll advice. Always follow your agency's or state's payroll and reporting requirements.
