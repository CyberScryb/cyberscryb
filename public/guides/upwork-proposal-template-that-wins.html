Five-part structure

1) Mirror their goal in one sentence. 2) One relevant result you delivered. 3) How you would approach their job. 4) Timeline and deliverable. 5) Soft CTA (question or free audit).

What to cut

Life story intros, generic “I’m a hard worker” lines, and walls of skills without proof. Clients skim on mobile.

FAQ

How long should an Upwork proposal be?

Usually 120–250 words for the cover letter, plus a short relevant sample or plan if the job is complex.

Can AI write my whole proposal?

Use AI for a first pass, then rewrite in your voice with one concrete result only you could claim. Clients spot pure AI paste.
