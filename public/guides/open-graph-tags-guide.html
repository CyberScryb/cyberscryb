What Are Open Graph Tags?

Open Graph is the reason shared links show a nice title, description, and image instead of a bare URL. Facebook created the standard, and now everyone uses it. Without OG tags, platforms just guess — and they guess wrong a lot.

Essential Open Graph Tags

  • og:title — The title displayed in the social share card
  • og:description — A brief summary (2-4 sentences)
  • og:image — The preview image (1200×630px recommended)
  • og:url — The canonical URL of the page
  • og:type — Usually "website" or "article"
  • og:site_name — Your website's name

Frequently Asked Questions

What image size should I use for og:image?

Make your share image 1200×630 pixels. That's the size Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter all handle well — one image, no surprises.

Do I need separate tags for Twitter?

Twitter has its own card tags, but here's the good news: if you skip them, Twitter falls back to your OG tags. So OG tags alone cover you in most cases.
