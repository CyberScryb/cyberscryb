What's in the box

Everything you want. Nothing you don't.

Built feature by feature around actual reading. No account. No subscription. No upsell.

Powerful Extraction

Powered by Mozilla Readability — the same engine behind Firefox Reader View. Headings, lists, code blocks, blockquotes, and figures all come through with structure intact. Works on news sites, blogs, Substack, Medium, Wikipedia.

Three Thoughtful Themes

Light, sepia, and a refined dark mode (no harsh pure black). Cycle through them with a single keypress. Your choice sticks.

Typography You Control

Three font families including a dyslexia-friendly option. Four sizes. Three column widths. Three line heights. Dial it in once and it stays across every article.

Read Aloud, Properly

Sentence-level highlighting follows as it speaks. Pick any system voice. Speed slider from 0.5x to 2x. Hands-free reading that actually tracks where you are.

Reading Library

Bookmark articles to a local library. Read them back from the popup, even offline. Up to 100 articles, capped automatically — no clutter building up.

Reading Position Memory

Come back to an article tomorrow and Distill drops you right where you left off. Per article, automatically.

Markdown Export

Copy or download the cleaned article as Markdown or plain text. Perfect for Obsidian, Notion, Bear, or any note-taking app that understands Markdown.

Print-Ready

Hit P (or the print icon) and you get a clean, paper-friendly page. No ads, no nav, no garbage bleeding into the margins.

Works entirely on your device

Distill doesn't send page content anywhere. It doesn't track you. It doesn't talk to any servers. Your settings and saved articles live in your browser's local storage — no account required, nothing leaves your machine.
